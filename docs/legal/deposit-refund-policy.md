# Schedule A — Security Deposit Settlement Policy — DRAFT

1. **Nature.** The deposit is a refundable liability of HSP. It is never treated as revenue and earns no interest.
2. **When settlement starts.** On termination or non-renewal, the franchise is first suspended on the platform. No
   settlement is raised while the franchise is active.
3. **Permitted deductions** — only these, each documented with evidence and an amount:
   a. HSP property, samples or display material not returned within 15 days, at documented cost;
   b. amounts the Franchisee owes HSP and that are not otherwise set off against commission;
   c. documented losses caused by the Franchisee's breach (e.g. a customer refund caused by the Franchisee's
      misrepresentation), after the Franchisee has had 7 days to respond in writing.
   Deductions can never exceed the deposit held. No deduction for loss of profit or for "non-performance" alone.
4. **Statement.** HSP sends the Franchisee a settlement statement: deposit held, each deduction and reason, refund due.
5. **Approval.** One HSP finance user raises the settlement; a **different** finance user approves it. Either may
   reject it if evidence is insufficient (system rule: maker-checker; enforced in the platform).
6. **Refund.** Within 30 days of termination (or 15 days after the property-return period, if later), by bank
   transfer to the Franchisee's verified account. The transfer reference (UTR) is recorded against the settlement.
7. **Disputes.** The Franchisee may dispute deductions within 15 days of the statement; undisputed amounts are paid
   without waiting.
8. **Records.** The settlement, approvals and reference are kept in HSP's audit trail for 8 years.

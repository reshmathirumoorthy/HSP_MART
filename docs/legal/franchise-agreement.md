# City Business Franchise Agreement — DRAFT

> Draft for legal review. Signed (physically or e-signed) before the franchise is activated on the platform.

**Between** HSP Office Basics Private Limited, CIN [CIN], GSTIN [GSTIN], [REGISTERED ADDRESS] ("**HSP**") and
[FRANCHISEE LEGAL NAME, CONSTITUTION, PAN, GSTIN, ADDRESS] ("**Franchisee**").

## 1. Appointment
1.1 HSP appoints the Franchisee as a non-exclusive City Business Partner for [CITY] ("**City**") to develop and
service customers for HSP Mart gifting products.
1.2 **HSP remains the seller of record** for every order: HSP sets and approves prices, issues invoices, collects
payment, procures from approved suppliers, and is accountable for fulfilment and complaints.
1.3 The Franchisee shall not procure substitutes, sell competing products under the HSP brand, collect customer
payments, or issue documents in HSP's name.

## 2. No entry fee; refundable deposit
2.1 No non-refundable entry fee is payable.
2.2 The Franchisee pays a **refundable security deposit** of ₹[25,000–1,00,000] to [BANK DETAILS FOR DEPOSITS].
HSP holds it as a **liability**, not income. It bears no interest.
2.3 The deposit secures the Franchisee's obligations (return of HSP property, dues, documented losses) and is
refunded under the Deposit Settlement Policy (Schedule A).
2.4 No inventory purchase is required unless separately agreed in writing.

## 3. Territory
3.1 Rights in the City are **non-exclusive** and **performance-based**. No territorial monopoly is granted in return
for the deposit.
3.2 HSP may designate national key accounts, which are served by HSP and are outside this Agreement.
3.3 Lead attribution follows the platform rules: 90-day protection for registered qualified leads, one partner per
order, contracted-account renewal rights as recorded by HSP.

## 4. Remuneration
4.1 **Commission** of 10% of the eligible base (net realised selling value excluding GST, freight, cancellations,
returns and credit notes) on attributed orders.
4.2 **Service fee** of 2% of the same base for orders where the Franchisee performs agreed local servicing
(customer meetings, sample handling, delivery coordination), subject to HSP's minimum contribution rules; HSP may
decline or reprice quotes that fall below its minimum margin.
4.3 Payment stages, set-off, TDS, KYC and payout controls are as in the Associate Agreement clauses 4.3–4.6, which
apply mutatis mutandis.
4.4 No payment is made for recruiting other partners.

## 5. Franchisee obligations
Maintain a local point of contact; respond to customer enquiries within one working day; follow HSP brand,
communication-consent and data-protection rules; log customer complaints on the platform; return HSP samples and
materials on request; maintain licences required for its own business.

## 6. Performance review
HSP reviews performance quarterly (leads, quotes, orders, contribution, complaints). If agreed minimums
[to be set in Schedule B] are not met for two consecutive quarters, HSP may convert the arrangement to the
Independent Associate programme or terminate under clause 7.

## 7. Term and termination
7.1 Initial term 12 months, renewing annually unless either party gives 30 days' notice.
7.2 HSP may suspend immediately for breach of clauses 1.3 or 5, fraud or data misuse, and terminate if not cured
within 15 days of notice.
7.3 On termination: Franchisee stops using HSP marks; returns HSP property; earned commission is paid as it becomes
payable; the deposit is settled under Schedule A.

## 8. General
Confidentiality survives 3 years. Liability cap: amounts paid under this Agreement in the preceding 12 months, except
fraud and data misuse. Disputes: negotiation, then arbitration by a sole arbitrator at [JURISDICTION CITY] under the
Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. Governing law: India.

**Schedule A** — Deposit Settlement Policy (see `deposit-refund-policy.md`).
**Schedule B** — Performance minimums [to be agreed per city].

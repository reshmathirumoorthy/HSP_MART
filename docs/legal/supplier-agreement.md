# Approved Supplier Agreement — DRAFT

**Between** HSP Office Basics Private Limited ("**HSP**") and [SUPPLIER LEGAL NAME, GSTIN, ADDRESS] ("**Supplier**").

1. **Approval.** Supplier may receive purchase orders only after HSP procurement approves it on the platform. HSP may
   suspend approval for quality, delivery or compliance failures.
2. **Rates and stock.** Supplier maintains its purchase rates, lead times and available stock on the HSP supplier
   portal and keeps them accurate. Rates are firm for 90 days unless agreed otherwise.
3. **Purchase orders.** Supplier acknowledges each PO on the portal within one working day and dispatches by the
   agreed date. Supplier never contacts HSP's customers directly or discloses HSP's selling prices; ship-to details
   are used only to deliver.
4. **Branding.** For branded orders, production starts only after HSP confirms customer-approved artwork and
   specification on the platform. Supplier bears the cost of rework for deviations from approved artwork.
5. **Quality.** Goods must match the approved sample and specification, be new, correctly labelled (including MRP and
   Legal Metrology declarations where applicable) and safely packed.
6. **Food products.** Supplier holds and maintains a valid FSSAI licence/registration [NUMBER], complies with FSS
   labelling and shelf-life rules, and supplies goods with at least [60]% remaining shelf life at dispatch.
7. **Defects and complaints.** HSP routes supply-defect complaints to Supplier on the portal. Supplier responds with
   corrective action within 2 working days and replaces or credits defective goods at its cost, including freight.
8. **Delivery proof.** Supplier provides dispatch details and proof of delivery for every shipment it delivers.
9. **Invoicing and payment.** Supplier invoices HSP (never the customer) with correct HSN and GST. HSP pays within
   [30] days of delivery and acceptance, net of credits for defects.
10. **Data.** Recipient names, addresses and phone numbers are HSP's customers' personal data: use only to deliver,
    do not retain beyond 90 days after delivery, and report any breach to HSP within 24 hours.
11. **Term.** 12 months, renewing; either party may end on 30 days' notice; open POs are completed.
12. **Liability and law.** Supplier indemnifies HSP for defective, unsafe or non-compliant goods. Governing law
    India; courts at [JURISDICTION CITY].

# Privacy Notice and Consent Wording — DRAFT (DPDP Act 2023 / DPDP Rules 2025)

## Notice (site footer, sign-in page and partner/supplier onboarding)

**HSP Office Basics Private Limited** ("HSP") is the Data Fiduciary for HSP Mart and HSP GiftOS.

**What we collect and why**

| Data | From | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name, email, mobile | You | Sign-in codes, order and quotation updates, support |
| Billing/shipping address, GSTIN | You | Delivery, tax invoices |
| Gift-recipient names, addresses, phones | Your organisation (bulk upload) | Delivering gifts you ordered — nothing else |
| Payment status (not card/UPI details) | Razorpay | Confirming and reconciling payments |
| Partner PAN, bank IFSC and last 4 digits, GSTIN | Partners | KYC, commission payouts, TDS |
| Messages and complaint details | You | Resolving complaints |
| Marketing preferences | You | Sending offers only if you opt in |

**Who receives it:** suppliers and couriers (delivery details only), Razorpay (payments), email/WhatsApp/SMS
providers (messages), our accountants and tax authorities (invoices, TDS), and the partner who referred you (your
name, order status and value, never payment details). We do not sell personal data.

**How long:** order and invoice records 8 years (tax law); recipient lists 90 days after delivery; marketing consent
until withdrawn; sign-in codes 10 minutes.

**Your rights:** access, correction, erasure (subject to legal retention), withdrawal of consent, grievance redressal
and nomination. Contact: [DPO / CONTACT]. If unresolved, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## Consent checkboxes (unticked by default)

- Checkout / account: "Send me offers and festive gifting ideas from HSP Mart by email and WhatsApp. I can opt out
  any time." *(Without this, the platform blocks marketing messages to you.)*
- Bulk recipient upload (corporate customer): "I confirm my organisation is permitted to share these recipients'
  details with HSP solely to deliver these gifts."
- Partner onboarding: "I agree to the Associate Agreement (HSP-PA-2026.1) and to HSP processing my PAN and bank
  details for KYC, commission payments and TDS."

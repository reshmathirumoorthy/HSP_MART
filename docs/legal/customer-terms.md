## Prices and payment
- Prices are shown excluding GST; GST is added at checkout and shown on the invoice.
- Delivery: ₹99 (plus GST) on orders below ₹999; free above.
- Online orders are paid in full through Razorpay. You must sign in to place an order.
- Custom and bulk orders: a quotation is valid until its expiry date. A 50% advance is due before production and the
  balance before dispatch, unless HSP has approved credit terms in writing. Advances are adjusted against the final
  invoice and refunded if HSP cancels.

## Branded and personalised orders
Production starts only after you approve the artwork and specification. Approved personalised goods cannot be returned
unless defective or not as approved.

## Cancellation
- Online orders: cancel free of charge before dispatch.
- Custom orders: free before production approval; after that, costs already incurred may be deducted, with
  a statement.

## Returns and refunds
- Report damaged, missing, wrong or defective items within **7 days of delivery** on your order page
  ("Report a problem") or via the grievance officer.
- HSP will replace, issue a credit note, or refund to the original payment method. Refunds are initiated within
  7 days of approval.
- Food items: report within 48 hours of delivery; sealed, unopened food cannot otherwise be returned.

## Complaints and grievance officer
- Log complaints on your order page. We acknowledge immediately and aim to resolve within **3 working days**.
- Grievance Officer: [NAME], [DESIGNATION], [EMAIL], [PHONE], [ADDRESS]. We acknowledge complaints within
  48 hours and resolve them within one month, as the E-Commerce Rules require.

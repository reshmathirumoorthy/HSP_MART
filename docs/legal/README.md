2. Is the City Business arrangement a franchise or a commission agency for contract, tax and dispute purposes?
3. Is a refundable security deposit enforceable as drafted, and is the deduction schedule reasonable?
4. Confirm the TDS section and rate on commission under the Income-tax Act, 2025 (s.393) — the platform withholds
   2% (`COMMISSION_TDS_BPS=200`) pending confirmation.
5. Food hampers: confirm whether HSP needs its own FSSAI licence/registration in addition to suppliers' licences.
6. DPDP: confirm the lawful basis for processing gift-recipient data supplied by corporate customers.

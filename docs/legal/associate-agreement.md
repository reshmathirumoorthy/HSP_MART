# Independent Associate Agreement — DRAFT (version HSP-PA-2026.1)

> Draft for legal review. Accepted electronically when an applicant ticks the agreement box at `/join`; the platform
> records the version and timestamp against the partner record.

**Between** HSP Office Basics Private Limited, CIN [CIN], [REGISTERED ADDRESS] ("**HSP**"), and the person
named in the online application ("**Associate**").

## 1. Relationship
1.1 The Associate is an independent contractor who introduces customers to HSP Mart. Nothing in this Agreement
creates employment, partnership, agency to bind HSP, or a franchise.
1.2 **HSP is the seller.** HSP issues every quotation, tax invoice and credit note, collects every payment, procures
and fulfils every order, and is accountable to the customer. The Associate must not accept money from customers,
issue invoices, quote prices other than those generated by the HSP platform, or source substitute products under
the HSP brand.

## 2. No fees, no stock, no recruitment earnings
2.1 There is no joining fee, security deposit or minimum purchase.
2.2 The Associate is never required to buy inventory or samples.
2.3 The Associate earns **only** on eligible sales to customers. No amount is paid for recruiting other associates.

## 3. Leads and attribution
3.1 The Associate registers a prospective customer on the platform. A qualified lead is protected for **90 days**
from registration; during that period orders from that customer (identified by email, or company email domain for
organisations) are attributed to the Associate, including orders HSP handles directly.
3.2 One partner is attributed per order. Earliest valid registration wins.
3.3 Accounts designated by HSP as **national key accounts** cannot be registered as leads.
3.4 Where a protected lead converts and the customer enters a contract, HSP may record a contracted account in the
Associate's name for a stated term, carrying a **renewal commission** (default 5%, never above 10%) on repeat orders
while the Associate continues to service the account. HSP may end it for documented non-servicing.
3.5 The Associate must have the customer's permission to share their contact details with HSP.

## 4. Commission
4.1 Rate: **10%** of the eligible base, unless a different rate is stated on the platform for that order.
4.2 **Eligible base:** net realised selling value of goods and services, **excluding** GST, freight and delivery
charges, cancelled items, returns and credit notes, and pass-through charges HSP has specified.
*Example:* eligible sale ₹1,00,000 before GST → commission ₹10,000 before TDS.
4.3 **Stages** shown on the partner dashboard:
- *Pending* — order confirmed;
- *Earned* — customer has paid in full, order delivered, and payments reconciled;
- *Approved* — after the return window (currently 7 days from delivery) and only if the Associate's identity (PAN)
  and bank account are verified;
- *Paid* — transferred to the Associate's verified bank account.
4.4 If an order is cancelled, commission is reversed. If a credit note is issued, commission is reduced in proportion
to the credited taxable value; amounts already paid may be set off against future commission.
4.5 HSP deducts tax at source as required by law (currently 2%, subject to confirmation under the Income-tax Act,
2025) and issues TDS certificates.
4.6 Payouts are approved by two HSP finance users and made to the verified bank account only.

## 5. Conduct
5.1 Make no claim about products, prices, delivery dates or earnings that is not stated by HSP.
5.2 Do not send bulk promotional messages in HSP's name. Customer communications that require consent are sent by
HSP's systems.
5.3 Keep customer information confidential; use it only to serve that customer through HSP; delete it on request
or on termination. The Associate cannot see other partners' customers.
5.4 Use HSP's name and marks only as provided by HSP.
5.5 Complaints: help the customer log them on the platform (`/partner/complaints`). HSP resolves them.

## 6. KYC
The Associate provides PAN, bank account (IFSC and account number) and, if registered, GSTIN. Commission is not
approved until KYC is verified. The Associate must keep these details current.

## 7. Term and termination
7.1 Either party may end this Agreement by 15 days' written notice (email suffices).
7.2 HSP may suspend or terminate immediately for fraud, misrepresentation, collecting customer money, data misuse
or breach of clause 5.
7.3 On termination, Earned commission on orders completed under clause 4 remains payable; lead protection and
contracted-account rights end.

## 8. General
Liability of either party is limited to commission paid or payable in the preceding 12 months, except for fraud,
data misuse or breach of confidentiality. Governing law: India. Courts at [JURISDICTION CITY]. HSP may update this
Agreement with 30 days' notice; the platform records the accepted version.

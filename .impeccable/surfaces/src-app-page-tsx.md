# Storefront (home + shared shell): HSP Gifting House

Scope: home page plus the shared shell (header, footer, product card, listing, PDP chrome, quote sheet) that every route inherits. Mode: Persuade (home); listing and PDP lean Operate inside the same world.

Audience: corporate buyers (HR, admin, procurement) shortlisting bulk gifts; individuals secondary. Action: request a quote (quote sheet) or buy. Proof: real products and photography, real prices, contact and store details. There are no testimonials or client logos, and none may be invented.

History: the "trade catalogue" direction (seed 013da47f) was built, reviewed and then rejected by the user as not modern or premium enough. The user chose the category standard, a premium gifting house in the spirit of Ferns N Petals and Neiman Marcus. Convention is the commitment, executed straight at their craft level.

## Direction contract

THESIS: HSP Mart as a luxury corporate gifting house, where every hamper is presented like an unboxed gift: rich, calm, assured. It refuses the cluttered marketplace grid with badges, banners and discount noise.

OWN-WORLD: deep emerald fields (#0E3B2E / #082A20) and champagne-ivory paper (#FBF8F2 / #F3EDE2), with antique gold (#B98A3E) as hairline foil rules and the single accent for primary actions. Bodoni Moda (high-contrast didone) for display, prices and numerals; Jost (geometric sans) for text and small letterspaced labels on controls. Soft 6–10px corners, generous whitespace, photography on warm ivory, thin gold rules framing key moments.

STORY: the visitor feels HSP gifts are premium and handled with care (in-house curation and packing). They see real hampers at scale, understand corporate, festive and wedding paths, and act by adding gifts to a quote request or buying.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a full-width emerald field with a fine inset gold frame. On the left, a serif display headline ("The art of corporate gifting"), a short line and two actions: gold "Explore corporate gifts" and outline "Request a quote". On the right, a tall arched photo window with a real hamper and a small caption card. Below the fold line, an occasion row of four serif links with counts.

FORM: category standard (canon), chosen by the user; no seed roll.

Signature interaction: the Quote Sheet keeps its function, restyled as a "Quote request" list with a gold-edged tab. Adding a gift shows a gold check. Motion is soft fades and lifts with exponential ease-out and no overshoot.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
